# Things to consider:

1. Our target is university students attending an event
- Users shouldn't have to learn how cryptocurrency works before they can use the product.
- They’re not really interested in blockchain and few would even have a crypto wallet
1. The flow:

![image.png](Web3%20-%20Tasks%20as%20of%20now/image.png)

- The attendee signs in using email or Google.
- The application creates or associates their embedded wallet.
- The attendee claims a credential.
- Our platform sponsors the eligible minting transaction.
- The attendee presents their credential without needing to pay transaction fees.
